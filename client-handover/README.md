# What Larkfield Power sent us

Larkfield Power is a **fictional** regional electricity company in the United States, and every file in this folder is synthetic. It was generated by `tools/make_client_data.py`. The mess in it is on purpose.

## The email that started it

> **From:** Rachel Morgan, Head of Customer Operations, Larkfield Power
> **Subject:** Chatbot for our website
>
> Hi,
>
> We'd like a chatbot on our website so customers can check outages themselves.
> Our call center can't cope on storm days, and last Tuesday we had over 4,000
> calls in one evening. I've attached the exports our IT team could pull together,
> plus our policy documents. Daniel (IT) can give you access to the grid status
> service on the call.
>
> Can we talk on Thursday?
>
> Rachel

## What's attached

| File | What it is | What's wrong with it (we find out in the course) |
|---|---|---|
| `data/accounts.csv` | customer accounts and the area each one is in | duplicates, leading zeros, missing areas |
| `data/north_outages.csv` | outage log from the North division | US `MM/DD/YYYY` dates with AM/PM, area code and name in one column |
| `data/south_outages.csv` | outage log from the South division | ISO dates, different column names, a ticket entered twice, a missing end time |
| `data/crew_roster.csv` | field crews and their shifts | |
| `data/complaints.csv` | recent customer complaints | phone numbers inside free text, and one message that tries to give orders to an AI |
| `policies/*.pdf` | compensation policy, planned maintenance notice, storm safety advice | |
| `grid_api/` | a stand-in for their live grid status service | slow, rate limited, sometimes down |
| `call-transcript-kickoff.md` | the Thursday kickoff call | |
